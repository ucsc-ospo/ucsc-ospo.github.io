We are excited to share the progress of the [2026 cohort](/post/osre2026cohort/) of the Open Source Research Experience (OSRE) program, which includes participants in the Summer of Reproducibility (SoR) and Google Summer of Code (GSoC). 

Check out all the posts on the [OSRE 2026 Student Page](/osre26/#studentpages).

As part of the OSRE program, participants are required to post blogs to the program website three times during their summer program: an introductory blog to introduce their project, a mid-point blog about the status of the project, and a final blog at the completion of the program. 

Follow along this summer as contributors share progress updates on their projects via the OSRE blog. **Shout out to all the [mentors](/authors/) who are generously sharing their time and expertise to guide the next generation of open source researchers.**
