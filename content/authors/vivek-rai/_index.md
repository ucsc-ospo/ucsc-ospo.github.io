Vivek Rai is a graduate student in Computer Science at Illinois Institute of Technology in Chicago, focusing on efficient and decentralized ML systems. He has worked on perception and reinforcement learning pipelines for autonomous vehicles and is interested in moving into scientific computing and HPC. As a 2026 Summer of Reproducibility fellow with the [Chameleon](https://www.chameleoncloud.org/) project, he built an allocation-aware resource advisor and a benchmark for LLM-assisted testbed guidance.
