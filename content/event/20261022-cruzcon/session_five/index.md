This closing session invites everyone in the room to weigh in on how academic Open Source Program Offices (OSPOs) can help support infrastructure that enables long-term impact of research and open source built on campuses. Together, the group will look at what proper maintenance of research software requires, where OSPOs can connect existing campus resources, and how that support translates into greater reach and impact for university research.
